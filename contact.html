Contact

Contact Game520 News Hub

Questions, corrections, copyright concerns, and practical editorial suggestions are welcome.

How to contact the site

Game520 News Hub is written and maintained by James Chen. Email is the best way to reach the site because it creates a clear record for corrections, copyright review, and reader feedback.

  • General questions: contact@game520game.com
  • Corrections: include the article URL and the sentence that needs review.
  • Copyright concerns: include the page URL and the material in question.
  • Editorial suggestions: explain the engineering problem readers need help solving.

Response expectations

I aim to review messages within a reasonable timeframe, usually within a few business days. Corrections and copyright concerns are prioritized over general topic suggestions because they affect site accuracy and trust.

If your message requires outside verification or source review, a response may take longer. Sending the relevant URL, the exact sentence or material at issue, and a short explanation will make the review faster.

Correction handling

Correction requests are reviewed against the article text and available evidence. When a factual error is confirmed, the relevant page is updated. If the issue is a matter of interpretation, the article may be clarified rather than rewritten.

Game520 News Hub does not guarantee that every suggestion will become an article or that every disputed interpretation will be changed, but good-faith correction reports are taken seriously.

What not to send

Please do not send confidential source code, private credentials, unreleased customer data, or personal information that is not necessary for the request. The site is not a consulting service and cannot provide legal, financial, security, or individualized professional advice.
