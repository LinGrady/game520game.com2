Privacy Policy

How Game520 News Hub handles data, cookies, analytics, advertising, and contact emails.

Last Updated: May 6, 2026

Game520 News Hub is an independent technology practice site written by James Chen. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when readers visit the site or contact the site by email.

Information we collect

The site may collect basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring pages, pages visited, approximate location derived from network data, and date or time of access. This information is used to understand site performance, detect abuse, and improve content planning.

Game520 News Hub does not ask readers to create accounts and does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through the website.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. They may be used to remember preferences, measure visits, understand traffic patterns, support analytics, and help advertising systems operate.

Google Analytics

Game520 News Hub uses Google Analytics to understand aggregated site usage, including which pages are visited and how readers find the site. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to collect usage information. Reports are used to improve the usefulness and reliability of the site.

Google AdSense

Game520 News Hub uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Google uses cookies to serve ads, limit repeated ads, measure ad performance, and help provide ads that may be more relevant to users. Users can manage ad personalization through their Google settings.

Advertising partners may receive technical information from a browser when ads are requested. Game520 News Hub does not control all cookies or similar technologies used by third-party advertising systems.

Third-party images and external resources

Some pages may load images, fonts, scripts, embeds, or other resources from third-party services. Those external services may receive standard technical information such as IP address, browser details, and the referring page as part of the request required to load the resource.

Contact email data

If you email contact@game520game.com, the message may include your email address, name, organization, article URL, and the content of your request. This information is used to respond to the message, review corrections, evaluate copyright concerns, and maintain a record of site-related communication.

Please do not send confidential credentials, private customer data, or sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the issue you are raising.

How users can control cookies

Users can control cookies through browser settings. Most browsers allow users to block cookies, delete existing cookies, or receive warnings before cookies are stored. Blocking cookies may affect analytics, advertising behavior, or some website features.

Users can also manage ad personalization through Google settings and related advertising controls provided by their browser, device, or platform.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the site changes its analytics, advertising, contact, or data-handling practices. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to contact@game520game.com.
