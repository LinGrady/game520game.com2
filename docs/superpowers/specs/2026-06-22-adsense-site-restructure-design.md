# AdSense Site Restructure Design

Date: 2026-06-22

## Goal

Refresh Game520 News Hub into a focused software-engineering practice publication with clearer structure, less template residue, and stronger active-maintenance signals.

## Direction

Keep the static HTML structure, existing domain, and James Chen author model. Replace public-facing news-style language with engineering decision paths. Add recent practical articles and rebuild discovery pages around the strongest engineering clusters.

## Scope

- Do not modify `ads.txt`.
- Rebuild `index.html`, `blog.html`, and `categories.html` around practical engineering clusters.
- Refresh author, about, and contact pages where needed.
- Add recent articles that read like engineering notes from practice.
- Update `sitemap.xml`.
- Leave older generic article files in the repository but remove them from primary public entry points.

## Public Positioning

Game520 News Hub is an independent engineering practice publication by James Chen. It covers AI engineering, cloud architecture, security engineering, developer productivity, and technology strategy through practical trade-offs rather than broad technology news.
